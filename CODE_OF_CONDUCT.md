# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

bingsu follows the spirit of the [Rust Code of Conduct](https://www.rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct).

We want bingsu to be a friendly, safe and welcoming place for everyone, regardless of level of experience,
gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race,
ethnicity, age, religion, nationality or any other similar characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and courteous.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other
channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **<commander@ai-scream.ai>**.
All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they consider harmful.
